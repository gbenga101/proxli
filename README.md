# Proxli

Proxli is a Nigerian local service discovery platform that connects everyday people with nearby skilled technicians and artisans online.

## The Problem

Finding a reliable local technician in Nigeria — a phone repairer, electrician, plumber, or carpenter — is still largely dependent on word-of-mouth and referrals. Skilled artisans exist in every neighbourhood but have zero online presence. When something breaks, most people are stranded.

## The Solution

ServiFind makes local service providers searchable, contactable, and rateable online. Customers search by category and location, view provider profiles, and contact them directly via WhatsApp or phone. No payment gateway — just fast, trusted discovery and connection.

## Who It Is For

- Everyday Nigerians and households who need quick access to local help
- Skilled tradespeople and artisans who want online visibility without the complexity

## Service Categories

Phone Repairers, Electricians, Carpenters, Hair Stylists, Barbers, Tailors, Plumbers, Generator and AC Technicians, Painters, Welders, Tilers, TV and Electronics Repairers, Laundry and Dry Cleaning.

## Key Features

- Search providers by category and location
- Filter by rating, verified badge, response channel and price range
- Provider profiles with ratings and reviews
- Click-to-contact via WhatsApp or phone — no plain text numbers exposed
- Provider verification system with admin approval
- Full admin dashboard for platform management

## Launch Market

Badagry, Lagos State — expanding to Abeokuta and beyond.

## Tech Stack

- Frontend: Next.js + Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Node.js + Express.js
- Database: PostgreSQL + Prisma
- Hosting: Vercel (frontend) + Render (backend and database)

## Status

Currently in active development — MVP sprint in progress.
